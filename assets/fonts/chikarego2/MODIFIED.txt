Modified by consisTerm
======================

The font file here is not the original: the Latin-1 accented letters it
lacked (a/e/i/o/u with grave, acute, circumflex, tilde and diaeresis, n
with tilde, y with acute, c with cedilla, and their capitals) were added,
each drawn as the base letter plus the accent in squares of the font's own
pixel grid. Without them egui fell back to Arial for every accented letter
in a Portuguese string, at a different size and weight from the letters
around it. Nothing else in the font was changed.

The change was made with ../add_accents.py:

    python add_accents.py original.ttf modified.ttf [pixel size in units]

(64 units for ChiKareGo2 and FindersKeepers, 4096/11 for MS Sans Serif.)
The modified font is distributed under the same licence as the original.
